# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in BOSC Community Library a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and technical experience levels.
- Give and receive constructive feedback with care.
- Prioritize community safety, privacy, and trust.
- Credit contributors and external sources accurately.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, issues, reviews, or community spaces.
- Misrepresenting technical results, security claims, or contributor identity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and focused on restoring a healthy collaboration environment.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to repository maintainers through a private channel when available. Include relevant links, screenshots, dates, and a short description of the impact. Maintainers will review reports promptly and protect reporter privacy as much as possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, events, and public spaces where someone represents the project.
